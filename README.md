Thesis Summary
This project investigates whether algorithmic trading systems, specifically momentum and mean reversion strategies, can outperform traditional passive investing in the Nepalese stock market. It uses a synthetic market regime simulator to test robustness under varying market conditions, allowing a more realistic evaluation of strategy performance.

## 📘 Project Overview

This research project is part of my Bachelor's thesis, focusing on the design and evaluation of rule-based trading systems for the Nepalese stock market. The goal is to test the effectiveness of quantitative strategies using backtesting and simulation techniques, and to compare them against a benchmark buy-and-hold approach.

The backtester is designed with the following features:
- Dual-strategy system (Momentum & Mean Reversion)
- Dynamic capital allocation and trade sizing
- Monte Carlo simulation of trade outcomes
- Performance metrics: CAGR, drawdowns, profit factor, and more
- Synthetic regime simulation (bull, bear, sideways)
